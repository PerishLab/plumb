# structure.seat-member

## Trigger

A governed seat holds too many members, a member has the wrong name or content,
or a bounded leaf exceeds the bytes declared by its selected rule.

## Solution

Read the condition named by the finding. For membership or naming, decide
whether the repository belongs in an existing seat, the declaration owes a
narrower truthful rule, or the shape is real but not yet judged. Never widen a
seat or rule merely to admit the current tree.

For an over-cap leaf, move material to the authority that owns it before cutting
content. Command grammar belongs to `--help`, standing law belongs to
`plumb rule`, and complex recovery belongs here. Do not raise the cap merely to
fit prose whose authority already exists elsewhere.

## Evidence

The former catch-all seat entry replaced shape-specific escape hatches, while
the former wayfinder entry removed repeated command and law lists from agent
briefs. Both are branches of the same seat-member finding.

## EXIT

Remove this entry when every seat-member finding can state its complete move, or when the seat-member law retires.
