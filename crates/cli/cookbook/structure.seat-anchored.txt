# structure.seat-anchored

## Trigger

A member of a governed seat carries none of the anchors that identify that
seat's real members.

## Solution

Remove or relocate a foreign member, add the genuinely missing anchor to the
member, or correct the seat declaration when the anchor no longer describes
the exercised shape. Use an empty anchor set only when the level is deliberately
governed but its members are not yet judged.

## Evidence

Anchors distinguish a real member from an arbitrary directory without encoding
product-specific names in the checker.

## EXIT

Remove this entry when the finding can prove which anchor action is correct, or when the seat-anchored law retires.
