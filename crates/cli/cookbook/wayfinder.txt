# wayfinder

## Trigger

A brief is over the cap its seat declares.

## Move

Cut what answers to another authority before cutting anything else. A command
list belongs to `--help`, a standing list of laws belongs to `plumb rule list`,
and a scenario belongs to a cookbook entry a finding can name. What stays is the
object model, the boundary where the brief goes silent, and where to ask. Do not
raise the cap to fit the prose: the cap is a row in the rule set precisely so it
can move, and a round of polishing that only moves it has polished nothing.

A brief is read by a coding agent as authority, not skimmed by a person. Its
size is the size of the drift surface, and a restated law that has since moved
is an agent working from a false premise.

## Evidence

This repository's own brief was three times its cap. Ninety six of its hundred
and fifty eight lines were a command list the file itself called the binary's
and a standing list the file itself said not to copy.

## EXIT

Remove this entry when a brief cannot restate another authority, or when the wayfinder cap retires.
