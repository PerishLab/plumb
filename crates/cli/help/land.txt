Project a clean topic branch onto its base and wait for its guard.

Forgejo is archived; land speaks to the GitHub repository origin names. It drives
the gh CLI, whose authority comes from the caller's Runseal profile
(runseal :liberte plumb land); Plumb holds no GitHub credential and owns no HTTP
sender of its own.

The source branch is kept. What lands is a one-commit projection at
land/<branch>. Plumb verifies the projection carries the staged-tree Guard proof,
posts that as the required guard status, and squash-merges the pull, bound to
the exact candidate the guard passed and carrying the candidate's own subject
and body, Guard proof trailer included. It then reads main back: the new head
must sit on the candidate's parent, hold the candidate's tree, and carry its
Guard proof, or land refuses and names the commits. A base that moved under the
merge leaves main holding a tree no Guard proved, which land reports and cannot
undo. The caller supplies the repository-local title and body explicitly; Issue
lifecycle orchestration belongs to Concord.
