Project a clean topic branch onto its base and wait for its guard.

Forgejo is archived; land speaks to the GitHub repository origin names. It drives
the gh CLI, whose authority comes from the caller's Runseal profile
(runseal :liberte plumb land); Plumb holds no GitHub credential and owns no HTTP
sender of its own.

What lands is a one-commit projection at land/<branch>. Plumb verifies the
projection carries the staged-tree Guard proof and posts it as an informational
guard status; that status is not what the organization ruleset requires. The
ruleset requires the organization Guard workflow run, so land waits, bounded to
300 seconds, for that run on the exact candidate to complete with success and
for the pull to read back at the candidate with mergeStateStatus CLEAN. Only
then does it squash-merge the pull, bound to the exact candidate and carrying
the candidate's own subject and body, Guard proof trailer included. A policy
refusal is retried only inside that wait; a failed run, a moved head or any
other merge state refuses. Land never merges with --admin or --auto. It then reads main back: the new head must sit on the candidate's
parent, hold the candidate's tree, and carry its Guard proof, or land refuses
and names the commits. A base that moved under the merge leaves main holding a
tree no Guard proved, which land reports and cannot undo. The caller supplies
the repository-local title and body explicitly; Issue lifecycle orchestration
belongs to Concord.

Once main is read back, land deletes the two branches it pushed, the
projection and the topic: on origin by lease on the head it pushed, and locally
while the branch still holds that head, detaching this checkout from the topic
first. A branch that moved after the push, or is checked out in another
worktree, is kept and named. A merge that is not read back deletes nothing.
