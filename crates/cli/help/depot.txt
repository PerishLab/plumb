Move a release's changelog and skill onto Depot through wharf.

Depot is written by wharf alone. Plumb consigns: it places one document in
wharf's yard, a private bucket whose objects expire after seven days, and
dispatches wharf's depot workflow to lodge it. The document is addressed by
the sha256 of its bytes and created only, so a consignment names exactly what
it carries. Wharf reads it by that digest, vets it again and only then writes
the generation, so the yard is never trusted.

Consign a changelog with --kind changelog and the prepared notes in --dir; the
notes are proved against the diff budget of --version first, as plumb
changelog --prove does. Consign a skill with --kind skill; it is taken from
skills/<product> in the marker's own tree, and wharf refuses one that differs.

The yard seat comes from PLUMB_YARD_ENDPOINT, PLUMB_YARD_BUCKET,
PLUMB_YARD_ACCESS_KEY_ID and PLUMB_YARD_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY or its _FILE
variant, which the developer profile carries: runseal :liberte plumb depot
consign. Dispatch drives the gh CLI under the same profile.

By default consign watches the lodge and then reads Depot back: it succeeds
only when the standing generation for the marker holds exactly what was
consigned. --no-watch returns once the lodge is dispatched.
