Prove the staged tree, exact CI HEAD, or one committed task boundary.

With no runtime selectors, guard keeps its local contract: it proves the staged
tree for commit attachment, or checks an explicit --base/--head/--write task
boundary.

For CI, declare both PLUMB_GUARD_STRENGTH=full and
PLUMB_GUARD_BOUNDARY=head. Full runs every applicable Guard action known by this
Plumb release. Head requires a clean worktree and proves the exact checked-out
HEAD commit and tree. Human and --json output bind both selectors, repository,
commit, tree, Plumb and Depot identities, platform, action inputs, and execution
worlds into one evidence digest.

Selectors are explicit and provider-neutral. CI, GITHUB_ACTIONS, and other
provider variables do not select or alter this contract. A partial, unknown, or
contradictory declaration is refused.
