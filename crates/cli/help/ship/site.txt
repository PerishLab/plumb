Project a declared site onto its edge.

A repository declares one site with apps/*/wrangler.jsonc, and Plumb derives its
package, assets, worker, routes, and fingerprint from the build. No repository
ship wrapper stands between them.

Authority enters only through PLUMB_SITE_TOKEN, PLUMB_SITE_ACCOUNT, and
PLUMB_SITE_DOMAIN. The token never enters an argument or a log: it reaches
Cloudflare through Runseal's in-process dialect.
