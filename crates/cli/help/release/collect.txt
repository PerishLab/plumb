Collect exact public source and formal Scene review inputs without executing repository or product code, tests, containers or publication.

--repository names one public active PerishLab repository. --commit requires its exact 40-digit lowercase Git commit. GitHub's native repository/owner, commit, complete recursive tree and regular blob identities are checked. The complete tracked-source projection records paths, modes, sizes and native Git object IDs; it is a review input, not extracted public-schema completeness or downloaded bytes of every source file. The product's plumb.toml blob bytes are checked against its native object ID before parsing its identity and existing Windtun Scene declaration.

The declared Scene follows its canonical first-party Depot channel. Its pointer must equal its version standing; the exact manifest and every object are verified, including executable modes. Discovery and source identity are checked again before a snapshot is returned. There is no checkout, historical-generation or missing-field fallback. Unsupported nonregular source blobs remain unknown. A complete tree is bounded to 16384 entries; Scene inventory to 2048 objects and 64 MiB in total; documents to 4 MiB.

subjects.json is the versioned plumb.release.subjects/v1 declaration carried as an ordinary Scene asset. It names the Scene producer and every indexed case exactly once. Cases explicitly name normative or diagnostic role, subjects, fixtures, preparation, operating conditions and assertion evidence paths. All fields are required. Normative subjects contribute coverage even when the case is not selected by default; preparation and fixture identities contribute none. The bundle2 case projection checks identities and manifest presence; it grants no execution admission. The Seatrial runner retains its complete bundle validation.

The shared library protocol owns parsing and snapshot construction. A material digest identifies inputs, not semantic equivalence. Source projection, all Scene materials and exact source/Scene identities remain review inputs; raw material changes do not automatically mean Y. Evidence-path existence does not authenticate the author's assertion about its meaning. A trusted bound review and baseline adoption remain separate obligations. Library custom transports own their authentication; in-memory fixtures prove no public source.

JSON is always emitted. observed/exit 0 means inputs were collected and a snapshot constructed, not that its boundary is complete, unchanged, qualified, adopted or authorized. Historical Scenes without subject metadata, absent declarations or absent normative coverage are unknown/exit 3 and retain collected facts. Invalid or conflicting material refuses/exit 1; unavailable reads remain unknown. adoption and publication are always false. This command does not activate a gate in open, stamp, ship or Wharf.
